Masterclass & SOP

Your company's way of working, turned into training.

We study the tasks your team does every day, then build a masterclass and SOPs made for your company. Every time you hire someone new, we train them with it, so they learn the job the right way from day one.

  1. We study

    We sit with your team and learn each task: the tools, the steps and the standards you expect.

  2. We build

    We turn it into a masterclass and step-by-step SOPs, written for your business and no one else's.

  3. We train

    Each new employee goes through the masterclass with us, using the SOPs as their guide, and we keep both updated as you grow.
