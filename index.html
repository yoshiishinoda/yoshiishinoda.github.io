About the founder

Hi, I'm Yoshi Ishinoda.

I have spent more than six years in operations and executive support: running businesses and business operations, managing projects, supporting a CEO directly and training frontline teams.

I started Ishinoda Inc. so others can experience what I love doing: taking work off their plate so they can focus on what matters most. We find the right people for your team, and we run and automate your business operations for you.

And because I know how hard it is to find truly good leather goods, we offer bespoke pieces made by a Japanese artisan under our own brand, BURGIS.
